# Problem Discovery: ShowZen

## Problem Statement
> Artistic producers and managers struggle with fragmented agenda management and manual financial workflows, leading to costly scheduling conflicts, delayed proposals, and lack of real-time financial visibility for multiple artists.

## Problem Deep Dive

### Who Has This Problem?
- **Primary persona:** Artistic Producers, Managers, and Booking Agents at representation offices (e.g., Divina Providência).
- **Secondary persona:** Artists themselves (who need clear visibility of their schedule) and Administrative/Financial staff.
- **Estimated number of people with this problem:** Thousands of independent and mid-sized artistic production companies in Brazil (Estimated market of 5k+ active agencies).

### Problem Characteristics
| Dimension | Rating | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Pain intensity (1-10) | 9/10 | PRD highlights "Detection of Conflicts" as a core pain point. Manual work causes billing errors. |
| Frequency | Daily | Agencies manage multiple artists with events occurring every weekend and negotiations daily. |
| Willingness to pay | High | Client (Divina Providência) has already invested R$ 10k for the initial system + site. |
| Urgency to solve | High | Scheduling conflicts lead to loss of reputation and legal/contractual penalties. |

### Current Workarounds
| Workaround | Cost/Time | Limitations |
|---|---|---|
| Excel/Google Sheets | 10+ hours/week | No automatic conflict detection; hard to share multi-artist views; human error. |
| WhatsApp Groups | Fragmented data | Information gets lost; no history; hard to extract financial data. |
| Manual PDF Creation | 1 hour/proposal | Slow response time to clients; inconsistent branding; no tracking. |

### Root Cause Analysis
- **Why does this problem exist?** Most ERPs are either too generic (CRM) or too complex and expensive, and they don't handle the specific "logistics vs. negotiation" flow of the music industry.
- **Why hasn't it been solved well yet?** The "middle market" of artistic production is underserved, caught between manual processes and enterprise-grade software that doesn't fit their specific workflow.
- **What's changing that makes a new solution viable now?** Increased digitization of the event industry in Brazil and the need for decentralized access (managers, roadies, artists) on a simplified interface (mobile/web).

## Ideal Solution (High Level)
> A centralized, visual platform that unifies the agendas of multiple artists with automatic conflict detection, integrates CRM for contract negotiation, and automates the generation of professional proposals and financial reports.

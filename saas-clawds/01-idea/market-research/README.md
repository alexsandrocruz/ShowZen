# Market Research: ShowZen

## Market Overview
The Brazilian artistic and entertainment market is one of the most vibrant in the world, with a strong emphasis on live performances (shows, festivals, regional events like "São João"). However, the management of these events is often handled manually by small to mid-sized agencies.

## Market Size & Sizing (TAM, SAM, SOM)
- **TAM (Total Addressable Market):** All entertainment and event production companies globally (~$1.5 trillion market).
- **SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market):** Artistic production and management companies in Brazil (~50k+ companies including small independent producers).
- **SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market):** Mid-sized artistic representation offices managing 2-10 professional artists (Estimated 5k agencies).

## Market Trends
- **Professionalization:** Increasing demand for transparency and professional reports from contractors (especially City Halls/Public Sector).
- **Digital Transformation:** Shift from manual spreadsheets to integrated SaaS for logistics and finance.
- **On-the-go Management:** Need for mobile-first views for roadies and artists during tours.

## Target Audience Segments
1. **The "Divina Providência" Persona:** Established offices managing real careers, high transaction volume, needing professionalization.
2. **Independent Managers:** Solo producers managing 1-2 artists, looking for affordable automation.
3. **Agencies for Corporate Events:** Focused on private branding events but needing the scheduling features.

## Market Dynamics
- **Drivers:** High demand for live music; growth of regional festivals; public sector spending on "festejos".
- **Barriers:** Inertia (resistance to change manual habits); budget constraints for smaller producers.

## Research Sources
- **ABRAPE (Associação Brasileira dos Promotores de Eventos):** Data on the growth of the event sector Post-Pandemic.
- **Statista / SEBRAE:** Reports on the creative economy and small businesses in the entertainment sector.

# Competitor Analysis: ShowZen

## Competitive Landscape
The market is currently split between manual workarounds and enterprise software that doesn't quite fit the artistic manager's workflow.

## Indirect Competitors (Status Quo)
1. **Excel/Google Sheets:** 
   - **Pros:** Free, flexible. 
   - **Cons:** No automation, high error rate, no real-time multi-user synchronization.
2. **WhatsApp Groups/Calendar:** 
   - **Pros:** Everyone has it. 
   - **Cons:** Fragmented, no reporting, hard to manage finances.

## Direct Competitors
1. **Generic CRMs (Pipedrive, Hubspot):**
   - **Gaps:** Don't understand "Event Date" vs. "Sale Date"; no native BRL currency automation; no agenda-specific views (consecutive shows, logistics).
2. **Event Management Software (Sige Eventos, Eventbrite):**
   - **Gaps:** Focused on ticket sales or venue management, not the artistic office's internal "Roadmap" and multiple artist availability.

## Competitive Advantages of ShowZen
- **Agenda-First UX:** Built around the visual calendar with native conflict detection.
- **Micro-SaaS Focus:** Tailored for the specific needs of artistic managers (commission tracking, tax for prefeituras).
- **Proposta Automation:** One-click PDF generation with the artist's branding (logo, colors, rider).
- **Local Context:** Compliant with Brazilian business rules and regional event dynamics.

## Competitive Roadmap Gap
| Feature | Manual/Generic | ShowZen |
|---|---|---|
| Automatic Conflict Detection | No | Yes |
| Artist-Specific Branding on PDFs | Limited | Native |
| Integration: Logistics & Finance | Hard | Seamless |
| BRL Tax Automations | No | Yes |

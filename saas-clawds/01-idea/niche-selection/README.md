# Niche Selection: ShowZen

## Niche Hypothesis
The ideal beachhead niche for ShowZen is **Artistic Representation Offices in Northeastern Brazil** that manage 3-10 active artists and have a high volume of "São João" and "Carnaval" event contracts.

## Why This Niche?
- **High Pain:** These offices deal with extreme scheduling pressure and complex logistics during peak seasons.
- **High Transaction Value:** Contracts with City Halls (Prefeituras) are often high-value but administratively heavy (requiring specific documentation and financial tracking).
- **Referral Loop:** This niche is tight-knit; success with one major office (like Divina Providência) provides strong social proof for others.
- **Underserved:** Existing ERPs are either too generic or too focused on the venue/client side, not the artist manager side.

## Niche Persona
- **Role:** Manager/Partner at the production office.
- **Key Motive:** Avoid embarrassing and costly agenda conflicts; professionalize proposals to increase closing rates.
- **Tech Level:** Comfortable with WhatsApp and basic web apps, but prefers simple, visual dashboards over complex forms.

## Competitiveness in Niche
- **Direct Competitors:** Generic event management tools.
- **Our Edge:** Native support for BRL currency, Brazilian tax calculations for public/private contracts, and a visual agenda designed for multiple artists simultaneously.

## Niche Scoring
| Criteria | Score (1-5) | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| Problem intensity | 5 | Scheduling errors are catastrophic. |
| Speed of sales | 3 | High seasonality; offices are busy but need solutions during off-peaks. |
| Ease of reach | 4 | Concentrated communities in regional hubs. |
| Potential LTV | 4 | Subscription-based with potential for "fee per contract" expansion. |
| **Total Score** | **16/20** | |

## Decision
**Beachhead Niche:** Artistic Management Agencies managing 3+ artists with a mix of public and private contracts.

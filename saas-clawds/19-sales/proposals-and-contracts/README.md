# Proposals and Contracts: ShowZen

## Sales Documentation Strategy
Every proposal generated by ShowZen should not just be a "cost estimate" but a professional document that speeds up the closing process between the Agency and the Event Contractor.

## Proposal Content Structure (BRL Standard)

1. **Brand Identity:** Agency Logo + Artist Visual Identity (Banner).
2. **Event Details:** Name, Date, Local, Capacity, Expected Audience.
3. **Artist Info:** Short Bio + Key Stats (Social Proof).
4. **Commercial Terms (Gatilhos de Venda):**
    - **Cachê Bruto:** Total value.
    - **Transporte/Hospedagem (Rider):** Responsabilidade do contratante vs. Incluso.
    - **Forma de Pagamento:** % na assinatura, % na data.
5. **Technical Requirements:** Link to the Tech Rider (Mapa de Palco/Input List).
6. **Legals:** Expirity date of the proposal.

## Contract Templates (Legal Roadmap)
We provide templates (pre-filled with metadata) for:
- **Contrato de Exclusividade (Agency <> Artist):** Defining commissions (10-20% standard).
- **Contrato de Performance (Agency <> Contractor):** Detailed clauses about cancelation, ECAD, and security.

## BRL Tax & Fee Integration
The proposals automatically calculate:
- **ISS (Imposto Sobre Serviços):** Based on the city of the event.
- **Internal Commission:** Calculating what stays with the office and what goes to the artist.
- **ECAD / Production Fees:** Notes on who pays these fees.

## Automated PDF Engine
- **Direct Export:** React components transformed into PDF (using QuestPDF/PDFMake).
- **Online Tracking:** (Post-MVP) A link sent to the client to notify the agent when the proposal is viewed.

# Sales Process: ShowZen

## Sales Motion
- **Type:** Sales-led (High-touch for agencies/offices).
- **Average deal size (ACV):** R$ 5.000 - R$ 20.000 (Setups + Monthly fees).
- **Average sales cycle:** 15-45 days.
- **Typical buyer persona:** Owner of Artistic Production Office, Financial Director.
- **Decision maker:** Office Owner.
- **Champion:** Booking Agent (suffers the most from agenda conflicts).

## Pipeline Stages

| Stage | Definition | Exit Criteria | Avg. Duration |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lead / Prospecting** | Identification of agency via social/network. | Contact details verified. | 2 days |
| **Qualified** | Preliminary chat to confirm number of artists (min 3). | Decision maker identified; pain confirmed. | 3 days |
| **Demo Scheduled** | Video call scheduled to show ShowZen live. | Calendar invite accepted. | 5 days |
| **Proposal Sent** | Commercial quote sent with specific artist volume. | Email confirmation of receipt. | 7 days |
| **Negotiation** | Discussion on pricing, taxes, and customization. | Terms agreed upon. | 10 days |
| **Closed Won** | Contract signed and first payment (setup) received. | Handoff to Onboarding. | - |
| **Closed Lost** | Deal fell through. | Reason documented (Price, Competitor, etc). | - |

## Qualification Criteria (BANT)

| Criterion | Question | Pass Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| **Budget** | "Vocês possuem orçamento alocado para a digitalização da gestão este ano?" | Flexibility to pay setup fee + monthly SaaS. |
| **Authority** | "Além de você, quem mais participa da decisão de contratação de ferramentas?" | Direct contact with the owner/owner. |
| **Need** | "Quantas vezes vocês já tiveram problemas de conflito de agenda nos últimos 6 meses?" | Managing 3+ artists; high pain in agenda/PDFs. |
| **Timeline** | "Quando vocês gostariam de estar com a casa em ordem e o sistema rodando?" | Ready to start implementation in < 30 days. |

## Lead Routing & Handoff
- **Inbound lead (Landing Page):** Automatically assigned to Founder for initial reach out.
- **Cold Outreach:** Sales assistant qualifies; Founder does the Demo.
- **Disqualify when:** Agency has < 3 artists (not enough complexity to justify the setup to them yet) or no budget for software.

# Demo Playbook: ShowZen

## Demo Flow (45 min)

| Segment | Time | Content | Key Message |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Intro + Agenda** | 5 min | Introductions and setting expectations. | ShowZen is built for producers by producers. |
| **Discovery** | 10 min | Understanding their artist roster, current pains (Excel/WhatsApp). | We understand the chaos of tour management. |
| **The "Source of Truth"** | 10 min | Showing the Multi-artist Agenda and centralized data. | Everything you need in one view. |
| **The Aha Moment** | 10 min | Booking a show and triggering a conflict + Generating PDF. | Precision and Speed. |
| **Pricing + FAQ** | 10 min | Cost breakdown, implementation time, BRL taxes. | Clear ROI and professionalization. |

## Aha Moment
> "O momento em que o produtor vê o alerta vermelho de conflito de agenda antes de fechar o show, e logo em seguida clica em um botão para gerar a proposta em PDF com o logotipo dele."

- **How to surface it:** Simulate booking a show for an artist who already has an "Event Lead" or "Confirmed Show" nearby.
- **When in demo:** Mid-point, right after showing the visual agenda.
- **Backup if it doesn't land:** Focus on the "Financial Automation" — showing the ISS/Tax calculation being done instantly.

| Competitor | Strength | Weakness | ShowZen Advantage |

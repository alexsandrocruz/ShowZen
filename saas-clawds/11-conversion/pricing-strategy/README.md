# Pricing Strategy: ShowZen

## Overview
ShowZen's pricing is built around the value of **centralized management**. As an agency grows in artists, the complexity (and risk of error) scales. Our pricing reflects this by using "Artistas Gerenciados" as the primary value metric.

## Value Metric Analysis
- **Metric:** Número de Artistas Ativos.
- **Why it works:** Managers only pay more when they have more business to manage. It's a "Success Metric" — more artists usually mean more revenue for the agency.
- **Metering:** Automated check on the number of non-archived artist profiles in the dashboard.

## Pricing Psychology

### 1. Anchoring
On the landing page, we highlight the **Growth (R$ 497)** plan as the "Ideal para Escritórios". It anchors the value between a cheap entry point and a high-end custom solution.

### 2. The Setup Fee (Onboarding Especializado)
- **Setup Fee:** Implementação inicial que garante o sucesso do cliente.
- **Propósito:** Cobre o custo de migração de dados das planilhas legadas, treinamento da equipe e configuração personalizada do workspace. 
- **Comprometimento:** Funciona como um filtro de qualificação e aumenta o LTV (Lifetime Value) ao reduzir o churn inicial.

### 3. Charm Pricing
Using `R$ X97` instead of `R$ X00` to align with standard Brazilian SaaS psychological benchmarks.

## Discounting Policy
- **Annual Plan:** Always 2 months free (~17% discount).
- **Early Adopter:** Locked-in 20% lifetime discount for the first 10 offices (Advocacy incentive).
- **NGO / Cultural Projects:** 50% discount for non-profit collectives.
- **Strict Rule:** Never discount the monthly price to close a common sale; instead, offer a longer free onboarding period.

## Competitor Benchmarking
- **Manual (Excel):** Cost is time and risk (High).
- **Generic CRMs:** ~R$ 100/user (but lacks artist-specific features).
- **Legacy ERPs:** R$ 1.000+ /mo (High complexity, low UX).
- **ShowZen Position:** Mid-market price with Premium UX and Niche specialization.

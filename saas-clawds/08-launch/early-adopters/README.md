# Early Adopters & Advocacy: ShowZen

## Strategy: The "Power of Referral"
Artistic production is a network-heavy industry. We will use the success of our founding client (Divina Providência) as a beachhead to attract the next 10 "Early Adopter" offices.

## Advocacy Program
We will identify "Champion" users within the early offices (Managers and Booking Agents) and provide them with:
1. **Founding Member Pricing:** Locked-in rates for the first 12 months.
2. **Direct Product Influence:** Priority in the feature roadmap (e.g., requesting specific integrations).
3. **Showcase Recognition:** Featuring their agency on the ShowZen/ZenSuite landing page.

## Testimonial Pipeline
- **Method:** Interview the Manager of Divina Providência after 30 days of full operation.
- **Key Quote Focus:** Impact on time saving and risk reduction (conflict prevention).
- **Format:** Short video testimonial + Written case study on the landing page.

## Referral Loop
- **Incentive:** Current users get 1 month free for every new agency they refer that signs an annual contract.
- **Target Channel:** Regional artistic producer associations (e.g., ABRAPE) and industry events.

## Target Early Adopter Persona
- Mid-sized agencies managing 3-10 artists.
- Offices that are growing and feel the "pain of manual scale".
- Producers who value professional branding and technology to differentiate themselves.
